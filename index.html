Your Journey Towards Better Movement Starts Here.

Pain and stiffness can change the way you work, walk, sleep and play. Physiotherapy looks at how your body is moving, finds what is holding you back, and builds a plan to help you move more comfortably again.

At Garishtha Physiotherapy Clinic in Miraj, the focus is on understanding your problem first, then guiding you through treatment and exercises that fit your daily life. Recovery is personal, so every plan starts with a conversation about what you want to get back to.

  • Care planned around your condition and goals
  • Support for pain relief, mobility and strength
  • Simple enquiry through WhatsApp or phone
